## Why this document exists, and how it differs

The existing reading — 63 sections — was built by **accretion**: a question was
asked, an instrument was applied, a section was written. That produces a
thorough document and a badly ordered one. It reaches for Shadbala early,
computes vargas at length before the houses they qualify, and arrives at
bhava-level judgments last.

**This document inverts that.** It follows one sequence and does not depart
from it:

> **1** what the bhava represents → **2** the bhava itself: sign, occupants,
> aspects → **3** the bhava lord → **4** the natural kāraka → **5** the
> relevant varga → **6** planetary strength → **7** bhava strength → **8**
> benefic/malefic influence, affliction, yogas → **9** dasha — *only now* →
> **10** Ashtakavarga and transit, last

Or, compressed: **what is promised** (bhava) → **who delivers it** (lord,
kāraka) → **how strong** (dignity, Shadbala, Bhava Bala, varga) → **when**
(dasha) → **how transit modifies it** (Ashtakavarga).

**No technique is assigned a percentage weight.** The bhava and its lord are
central; everything after qualifies, corroborates and times. Where a step adds
nothing, it is reported as adding nothing rather than padded.

**Every figure below is computed** — `verify_krama_all.py` produces the full
workup and nothing is asserted here that does not appear in its output.
